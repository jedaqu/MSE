## Scope

M1.10 establishes the first explicit backend-neutral persistence boundary derived from the behavior validated in M1.7–M1.9.

The new `PersistenceBackend` contract exposes logical block access, generation state, preparation, retained prepared state, publication, reconciliation, discard, and durability observation.

## Design boundary

The contract contains no host-file details such as paths, record formats, checksums, filesystem calls, or physical storage assumptions.

`FileBackend` implements the contract by delegating to its existing validated behavior. M1.9 semantics are therefore preserved rather than redefined.

`prepared()` returns an owned `PreparedState`, avoiding a requirement that future backends retain a borrowable in-memory representation of recovered state.
